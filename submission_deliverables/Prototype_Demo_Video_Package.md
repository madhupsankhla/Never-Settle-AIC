## C. Complete Voiceover Script

> *"In retail footwear, a sudden drop in store conversion often costs millions. But traditional dashboards only tell you what happened—never why.*
>
> *Meet SoleSight: the first deterministic root-cause analysis and decision intelligence engine purpose-built for store-based footwear retail.*
>
> *Powered by an in-memory DuckDB OLAP engine, SoleSight doesn't guess. In our Flagship Store scenario, walk-in footfall is up from advertising, yet conversion dropped twenty-four percent. SoleSight's statistical engine triangulates across twelve relational tables, executing dose-response regressions that mathematically prove a six-day stockout on core sizes UK eight and nine was the primary driver.*
>
> *Our Layer Four AI narrative engine translates this frozen mathematical evidence into role-tailored briefings for Store Managers, Regional Directors, Marketing Leads, and CFOs—complete with verifiable inline citations and feasibility-checked operational actions that recover thirteen point four lakhs in lost weekly revenue.*
>
> *Crucially, when telemetry is contradictory or low-confidence, our hardcoded Abstention Gate stops the AI from hallucinating, issuing an honest diagnostic hold instead.*
>
> *With an interactive Copilot, active learning feedback, and an automated verification suite, SoleSight bridges the gap between raw retail data and high-ROI operational execution.*
>
> *SoleSight: Intelligence to Action."*
