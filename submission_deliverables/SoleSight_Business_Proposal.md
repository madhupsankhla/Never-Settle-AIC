## 1. Executive Summary
The physical footwear retail industry faces a systemic, high-cost operational crisis: **conversion leakage caused by unmitigated size-curve imbalances**. While global footwear brands invest billions into top-of-funnel marketing campaigns, $20\%$ to $30\%$ of interested store walk-ins abandon purchases at the try-on bench due to stockouts in high-velocity core shoe sizes (UK 8 & 9). 

Traditional BI tools fail to connect store traffic drops to inventory distributions, while manual spreadsheet reporting takes 4–7 days—by which time revenue is lost forever. **SoleSight** delivers the industry’s first deterministic, automated Root-Cause Analysis and Decision Intelligence platform for retail footwear. By uniting POS, footfall sensors, inventory ERP, and staff rosters through an in-memory statistical causal engine, SoleSight recovers up to **₹13.4 Lakhs in lost weekly revenue per flagship store**. With a high-margin enterprise SaaS model, clear product-market fit, and a rapid 45-day ROI payback, SoleSight is positioned to capture a sizable share of the \$3.8B retail analytics market.

## 2. Market Opportunity & Problem Framing
* **The $38B Global Inventory Distortion:** Footwear is strictly anatomically constrained: a shopper who wears UK Size 9 cannot buy UK 7 or 11.
* **The Size-Curve Stockout Trap:** A single stockout in core sizes causes try-on conversion to drop by 24% while overhead footfall remains steady.
* **Delayed Return Spike:** Forced compromise purchases create a 34% size-related return spike 2–5 weeks later.
* **Information Silos:** Store managers, regional directors, marketing leads, and finance operate on disconnected data without a single causal source of truth.
